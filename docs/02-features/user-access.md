# Users

The Users page controls who can use Sauerobase. Access is by invitation only, and being added here is what lets a person sign in at all. Every user can use this page and gets full access to everything, this list included ([Rule 49](../01-business/business-rules.md)). There are no roles or permission levels (see [Roles and access](../01-business/roles.md)).

## Users screen: adding, editing and removing people

- **Add** a person by name and email address
- **Search** by name or email, and page through the list
- **Change** a person's name. The email address can't be changed, so remove the person and add them again
- **Remove** a person, which ends their access

Nothing records who added or removed whom, there's no suspend or deactivate (only removal), and there's no view of who's signed in or when someone last used Sauerobase.

## How someone gets in

1. An existing user adds their email address. No invitation email is sent, so tell the person yourself.
2. The person opens Sauerobase and signs in with a one-time code emailed to them, or with their Google account (same email address).

Someone who hasn't been added can't sign in either way and is told they're not in the system. There's no self-registration and no further approval step ([Rule 48](../01-business/business-rules.md)). Code and session details are in [Roles and access](../01-business/roles.md#getting-access).

A user can also connect Sauerobase to an AI assistant with the same sign-in (see [AI Assistant](./ai-assistant-access.md)).

## Removing a user

- It ends their access right away, including any AI assistant connection they set up ([Rule 51](../01-business/business-rules.md)).
- Their past work stays: SKU change history still reads as theirs, and demand plans they created or finalized are kept.
- You can't remove yourself ([Rule 50](../01-business/business-rules.md)).
