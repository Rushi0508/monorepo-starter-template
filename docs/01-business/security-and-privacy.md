# Security and privacy

## Sign-in

- There are no passwords. Each sign-in uses a 6-digit code sent to the person's email.
- A code works once, expires after 10 minutes, and allows 3 attempts.
- A new code can be requested once a minute.
- Only emails on the team list can request a code.

## Sessions

- After signing in, a person stays signed in for 30 days on that device. The session is
  renewed as they use the app.
- Signing out ends the session on that device straight away.
- Removing a person from the team ends all of their sessions.

## What data is stored

| Data | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Name and email of each team member | To identify people and send sign-in codes |
| Active sessions, with browser and IP address | To keep people signed in on each device |
| Training video titles, descriptions and links | To list videos in the Help Center; the videos stay on Loom or Tella |

## Who can see what

- The app and the Help Center are only available after signing in.
- Images used in Help Center pages are served publicly, so they must not contain customer
  or personal data.
