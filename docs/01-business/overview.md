# Product overview

The platform is a secure, web-based workspace for a team. People sign in with their work
email, see the tools their team uses, and find documentation and training in one place.

## Who it is for

- **Team members** use the app day to day.
- **Whoever manages access** keeps the team list up to date; today any team member can do
  this.
- **Stakeholders and clients** read this Help Center to understand what the product does,
  how access works and what is in scope.
- **Developers** extend the product; their pages are in the developer sections.

## What it does today

| Area | What people can do |
| --- | --- |
| Sign-in | Sign in with a one-time code sent to their email; no passwords to manage |
| Team access | See everyone who has access, add people, rename them and remove them |
| Help Center | Read guides, search them, and download any page as PDF, Word or Markdown |
| Training | Watch short Loom or Tella walkthroughs, and add new ones for the team |
| Navigation | Jump to any page, guide or video with the search bar (⌘K) |

## How it is delivered

The product runs in the browser on desktop and mobile; there is nothing to install. It has
two parts, the web app people use and the API behind it, which can be deployed together as
one container.
