# User access

## Who can sign in

Only people on the team list can sign in. Anyone else who enters their email is told they
have not been added, and no code is sent.

## Adding and removing people

Anyone already signed in can manage the team list from the **Users** page:

- **Add** a person with their name and work email. They can sign in straight away.
- **Rename** a person. Their email cannot be changed; to change it, remove the person and
  add them again with the new email.
- **Remove** a person. They are signed out of every device and can no longer sign in. You
  cannot remove yourself.

## What people can do

Everyone who can sign in has the same access: every page, the Help Center, and managing
users and training videos. There are no separate admin or read-only roles yet; see
[Scope and limitations](./limitations.md).

## The first user

A new installation has no users, so the first person is added directly to the database by
a developer. Everyone after that is added from the **Users** page.
